# v0.12.0 Release Notes

This update brings the community platform into the app: browse and discuss shared fits without leaving the app, share your own fits in one tap, and connect through the system proxy on Linux.

## Community Platform in the App

- New Community section to browse the shared-fit feed, read posts and comments, and join the discussion after signing in
- Filter the feed by popular ships or time window, and browse shared fits by ship with search
- Open a fit from a post directly into the fit view to import it into your workspace
- Upload actions now link to the publishing guide

## Sharing Fits

- Share a fit to the platform from the fit detail page
- Open the ship's platform page from the fit creation dialog to see what others have shared before creating your own

## Network

- On Linux, the app now follows the system proxy for chat, model discovery, and update checks
