# v0.17.0 Release Notes

This release shows how well a fit holds up against energy neutralization, displays drone damage directly on drone slots, and saves fit images straight to the photo gallery on mobile.

## Fitting

- Fit attributes now show capacitor warfare defense: energy neutralization resistance, the neutralization pressure needed to break the capacitor, and the energy needed to drain it — fits that cannot be broken show "Immune". These stats also appear on exported and shared fit pages
- Drone slots now show per-drone and total DPS and volley damage

## Fit Image Export

- Save fit images directly to the photo gallery on Android and iOS, or pick a location with a save dialog on Windows and Linux
- Show a clear error message when an image export fails
