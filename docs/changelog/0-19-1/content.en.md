# v0.19.1 Release Notes

This release fixes drone damage bonuses leaking onto fitted turret modules, which massively inflated turret DPS on drone boats.

## Fixes

- Fix ship drone damage bonuses and Drone Damage Amplifiers applying to fitted modules that happen to require the Drones skill (such as the Federation Navy 'Khryseos' Magnetic Field Stabilizer), multiplying their turret damage bonus by roughly 10x
