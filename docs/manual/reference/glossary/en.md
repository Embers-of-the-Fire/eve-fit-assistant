# Glossary

Quick definitions of terms used across the app and this manual.

## Fits and Slots

- **Fit** — a saved ship loadout of modules, drones, and rigs. See [Creating Your First Fit](efa://manual/getting-started/create-first-fit).
- **High / mid / low slots** — module slots for weapons, utility, and damage or tank modules. See [Modules and Slots](efa://manual/fitting/modules).
- **Rig slot** — slot for permanent ship modifications that consume calibration. See [Modules and Slots](efa://manual/fitting/modules).
- **Subsystem slot** — Tech 3 cruiser slots whose choices shape the hull's slot layout. See [Modules and Slots](efa://manual/fitting/modules).
- **Service slot** — structure slot for service modules such as cloning. See [Modules and Slots](efa://manual/fitting/modules).

## Modules and Modes

- **Tactical mode** — a switchable ship configuration on T3 destroyers. See [Tactical Modes](efa://manual/fitting/tactical-modes).
- **Online / active / overloaded** — module states: fitted and running, actively cycling, or pushed past rated limits. See [Overloading Modules](efa://manual/fitting/advanced/overloading).
- **Passive module** — a module that grants its bonus without being activated.
- **Damage profile** — the mix of EM, thermal, kinetic, and explosive damage used in calculations. See [Damage Profiles](efa://manual/fitting/damage-profiles).

## Characters and Items

- **Character** — a saved skill profile applied to fits. See [Managing Characters](efa://manual/characters/managing-characters).
- **Implant set** — a matched group of implants with set bonuses. See [Implants and Boosters](efa://manual/fitting/advanced/implants-boosters).
- **Booster** — a consumable that grants temporary bonuses with possible side effects. See [Implants and Boosters](efa://manual/fitting/advanced/implants-boosters).

## Data and Statistics

- **Channel / server / checkout / snapshot** — a channel offers servers; a checkout is your local copy of a data snapshot. See [Channels, Servers, and Checkouts](efa://manual/data/channels-servers-checkouts).
- **EHP** — effective hit points: raw HP adjusted by resistances. See [Attributes and Statistics](efa://manual/fitting/attributes-stats).
- **DPS** — damage per second. See [Attributes and Statistics](efa://manual/fitting/attributes-stats).
- **Capacitor stability** — whether the capacitor sustains all active modules indefinitely. See [Attributes and Statistics](efa://manual/fitting/attributes-stats).
