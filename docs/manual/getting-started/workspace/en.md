# Workspace and Fit List

The workspace is the app's home page. It shows a grid of shortcuts — create a fit, announcements, this manual, feedback, and settings — plus the list of your saved fits.

## The Fit List

Fits are listed by last-modified time. Pull down to refresh the list.

- **Tap** a fit to open it in the fitting page.
- **Long-press** a fit to view details of its ship.
- **Swipe right** to export the fit.
- **Swipe left** to delete it; deletion always asks for confirmation first.

A warning icon appears on a fit if it no longer matches the currently active game data (see [Channels, Servers & Checkouts](efa://manual/data/channels-servers-checkouts)).

## Creating a Fit

Tap **Create Fit** on the workspace to open the ship browser, then pick a ship and confirm to start a new fit. See [Creating Your First Fit](efa://manual/getting-started/create-first-fit).

## Data Update Banner

When newer game data is available, a banner appears at the top of the workspace. Tap **Update** to download it, or close the banner to update later from data settings. See [Updating Data](efa://manual/data/updating-data).
