# Feedback & Logs

Use Settings → **Report & Feedback** to open the report page. It has two tabs: **Bug Report** (title, summary, steps to reproduce, expected and actual behavior) and **Feature Request** (problem, proposal, impact). Optional app and device metadata can be included automatically to help diagnose issues. The link icon in the top bar lists external channels if you prefer to report there.

The app also occasionally asks how your experience is going; your answer can take you straight to the right report form, and you can dismiss the prompt permanently.

To attach logs to a report, enable [Developer Mode](efa://manual/settings/developer-mode) and open Developer Settings → **Collect Logs**. Filter log files by time range (last hour, 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, or all), select the relevant files, and share them — the app packs the selected logs into a single zip you can send along with your report.
