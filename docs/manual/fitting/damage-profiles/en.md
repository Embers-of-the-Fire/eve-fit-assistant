# Damage Profiles

Defense and EHP numbers depend on what is shooting at you. The incoming
damage profile sets the share of EM, thermal, kinetic, and explosive damage
your fit is assumed to take, and the HP panel combines it with your
resistances.

To change it, open the Attributes tab, find the damage profile row in the HP
panel, and tap the settings icon. Pick a preset from the dialog:

- **General** — uniform (25% of each type) or a single pure damage type.
- **Weapon ammo presets** — profiles matching real ammunition groups, such as
  frequency crystals, hybrid charges, projectile ammo, missiles, and bombs,
  useful when you know what a specific NPC or opponent deals.

The selected profile is saved with the fit and shown in the HP panel, so
anyone opening the fit sees tank numbers against the same damage mix.

See [Attributes and Statistics](efa://manual/fitting/attributes-stats) for the
panels these numbers feed into.
