# Drones and Fighters

Ships with a drone bay get a **Drone** tab on the fitting page; carriers and
other ships with fighter tubes get a **Fighter** tab instead. Tap the **+**
button to add an entry, then swipe the row to set the quantity — **x1** /
**x5** (or **Fill** for fighters) on the leading side and **+1** / **-1** /
**Delete** on the trailing side; with more than two actions on a side, the
extras fold into a **More** menu while **Delete** stays pinned at the outer
edge. The clear button
empties the whole bay.

Drone space is limited by two things: the volume of the drone bay and the
bandwidth, which caps how many drones can be in space at once. Toggle a row's
state to mark drones as active (in space, contributing DPS) or stowed in the
bay.

The Fighter tab header tracks light, support, and heavy squadrons against the
hull's per-category tube limits, plus the total tube count — counters turn red
when you exceed a limit. Fighters are always counted as active.

See [Attributes and Statistics](efa://manual/fitting/attributes-stats) for how
drone and fighter damage appears in the offense numbers.
