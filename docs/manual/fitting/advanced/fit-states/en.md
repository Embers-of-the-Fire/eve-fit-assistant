# Fit States

Every module and item in a fit has a state. Modules cycle through **online**,
**active**, and **overloaded**: online modules are fitted but not running,
active modules are running, and overloaded modules are running with the heat
bonus described in [Overloading](../overloading).

Tap a module to cycle its state up to its maximum supported state; tapping past
the maximum returns it to **passive** (disabled). Implants and boosters only
toggle between enabled and passive.

States drive the stats: only active modules consume capacitor and apply their
effects, while passive implants and boosters contribute nothing at all. Use
state changes to compare cap stability or damage with and without key modules.

See [Modules and Slots](efa://manual/fitting/modules) for slot basics.
