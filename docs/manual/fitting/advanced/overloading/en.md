# Overloading Modules

Explains how overheating modules boosts their performance at the cost of heat damage.

Overloading increases damage output, rate of fire, or active tank for a short
period of time. Keep an eye on the heat gauge — fully burnt modules go offline.

Heat spreads to neighboring modules in the same rack, so spread your most
important modules apart before a fight.
