# Fit Validation and Issues

The app continuously validates your fit and flags problems in place. Each
section of the fitting page — high, mid, low, rig, subsystem, service, drone,
fighter, implant, and booster — shows a warning or error icon in its title row
when something is wrong.

Tap the icon to open the issue list. Issues have two severities:

- **Errors** (red) — the fit cannot work as shown: unknown or missing item
  types, incompatible charge size or group, too many turrets or launchers,
  conflicting modules, wrong rig size, or modules that do not fit the hull.
- **Warnings** (amber) — the fit works but deserves attention, such as a
  weapon with no charge loaded.

Each issue names the affected slot and position so you can jump straight to
the problem module. Fix issues by swapping or removing the flagged item —
stats may be inaccurate until errors are resolved.

For fitting constraints that cause these errors, see
[Modules and Slots](efa://manual/fitting/modules).
