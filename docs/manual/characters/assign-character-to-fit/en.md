# Assigning a Character to a Fit

Every fit uses a character whose skill levels drive its computed stats — damage, tank, capacitor, and everything else on the stats panel. New fits start with the built-in All V profile.

To change it, open the fit and go to the character tab. The top row shows the current character; tap it to open a picker listing the built-in profiles and all your custom profiles. Switching characters recomputes the fit's stats immediately.

Skills come from the character, but implants and boosters are stored on the fit itself — they stay unchanged when you swap characters. The character tab has sub-tabs for both; see [Implants & Boosters](efa://manual/fitting/advanced/implants-boosters).

Because characters are shared, editing a skill level on a character updates every fit that uses it. Use separate characters (for example, an Alpha clone of your main) when you want to compare fits under different skill sets.

See [Attributes & Stats](efa://manual/fitting/attributes-stats) for how skills flow into each computed attribute.
