# Fit Price Estimates

See roughly what a fit costs on the market.

The estimated total appears at the end of the fit's attributes panel. Long-press the price to open a breakdown showing every item with its quantity, unit sell price, and unit buy price, plus sell and buy totals. The sell total is what you would pay to buy the whole fit; the buy total is what you would receive for selling it.

The estimate covers everything in the fit: the hull, all fitted modules and their charges, drones, fighters, implants, and boosters. Mutated (dynamic) items are priced at their resulting item type.

Prices come from the market server associated with your game data — Serenity or Tranquility — using data from ceve-market.org. If the data source declares no market server, a fallback can be set in the app settings; otherwise the feature stays hidden.

Items without an available price are silently left out of the total, so a partial figure can be optimistic. The breakdown page flags how many types could not be priced.
