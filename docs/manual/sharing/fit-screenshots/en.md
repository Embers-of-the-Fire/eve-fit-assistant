# Fit Screenshots

Render a fit as a single image you can save or share.

Open the screenshot view from the fit page. The app lays the fit out as a poster: implants and boosters in one column, all equipment (high/mid/low/rig/subsystem/service slots, drones, and fighters) in another, and the ship's attributes — capacitor, weapons, defenses, and cargo — in a third.

Tap **Save** to write the image as a PNG file to your Downloads folder. The file is named after the fit plus a timestamp.

Tap **Share** to send the image directly through the system share sheet to another app or contact.

The image is rendered at high resolution based on your device's pixel density, so it stays sharp when posted or zoomed. The fit must be fully loaded — including its calculated stats — before the screenshot can be captured.
