# Chat History

The chat history page lists every saved conversation, sorted by most recently updated first. Open it from the AI hub via **Chat History**, or from the **History** button in the chat page's app bar.

Each entry shows:

- **Title** — the first 30 characters of the conversation's first message (newlines are collapsed into spaces).
- **Time** — the last update, formatted `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM`.
- **Model** (when saved) — the model used for that conversation, shown at the end of the row.

Actions:

- **Tap an entry** — resumes the conversation and returns to the chat page so you can pick up where you left off.
- **Swipe left to delete** — swipe a row to the left and confirm to remove that conversation.
- **Clear conversations** — the app bar button deletes every conversation after confirmation and starts a new conversation.

With no conversations yet, the page shows "No conversations yet". Deleted conversations cannot be recovered, so confirm before deleting.
