# Fit Screenshot

The fit screenshot page renders the current fit as a poster-style image you can save or share. Open it by tapping **Export image** on the [Fitting Page](efa://manual/pages/fitting/fit)'s Utils tab.

## Preview

The body shows a live preview of the fit, laid out in a fixed three-column arrangement:

- **Character column** — implants and boosters.
- **Equipment column** — tactical mode, high/medium/low slots, rigs, subsystems, service slots, drones, and fighters.
- **Attribute column** — ship info, capacitor, weapons, resources, defense, misc, and cargo.

The preview is a read-only snapshot with no editing controls; the image can only be generated once the fit and its computed attributes have fully loaded.

## Save and Share

- **Save image** — writes a PNG file into the app's downloads (or documents) directory, named from the fit name plus a timestamp.
- **Share image** — sends the image through the system share sheet to other apps or contacts.

The image is rendered at a high resolution based on your device's pixel density, so it stays crisp when zoomed or published. For more on generating fit images, see [Fit Screenshots](efa://manual/sharing/fit-screenshots).
