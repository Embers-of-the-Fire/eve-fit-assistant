# Report Feedback

The Report & Feedback page (Settings → Report & Feedback) lets you send feedback to the development team. It has two tabs:

- **Bug Report** — fill in the title, summary, steps to reproduce, expected behavior, and actual behavior.
- **Feature Request** — fill in the title, problem, proposal, and impact, plus optional alternatives and extra notes.

Both forms end with an **Include metadata** checkbox (on by default) that attaches app and device information to help diagnose issues. When you finish, tap the submit button at the bottom. On success, a dialog shows the issue URL you can open in your browser; on failure, an error dialog appears and lists other external channels you can use.

The link icon in the top bar opens the [External Feedback Channels](efa://manual/pages/settings/report-external-links) list. To attach logs to a report, enable [Developer Mode](efa://manual/settings/developer-mode) and pack them on Developer Settings → **Collect Logs**. See [Feedback & Logs](efa://manual/settings/feedback-and-logs) for the full workflow.
