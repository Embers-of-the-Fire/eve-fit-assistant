# External Feedback Channels

The External Feedback Channels page lists ways to reach the team outside the app, grouped into sections. Open it from the link icon at the top of the [Report & Feedback](efa://manual/pages/settings/report-feedback) page.

- **General** — **GitHub Issues** (opens the project's issue tracker in your browser), **Tencent Form** (opens the feedback form), and **Tencent Sheet** (browse collected feedback data).
- **Community** — the **official QQ group**; tapping it copies the group number to the clipboard so you can search for it in QQ.
- **Security** — the **security email** (opens your mail client with the subject pre-filled) and the **security QQ** (tapping copies the number).

Tapping a link opens the corresponding page in a browser or app; group and contact numbers are copied to the clipboard. See [Feedback & Logs](efa://manual/settings/feedback-and-logs) for the full reporting workflow.
