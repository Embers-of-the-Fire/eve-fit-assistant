# Fit List

The Fits tab lists every fit saved on your device, newest by last-modified time first. Switch to it from the bottom navigation or by swiping the page.

## List Items

Each item shows the ship's icon, the fit name, the ship type, and the last-modified time.

- **Tap** a fit to open it in the fitting page. See [The Fitting Page](efa://manual/pages/fitting/fit).
- **Long-press** a fit to view the details of its ship. See [Item Detail](efa://manual/pages/fitting/item-detail).
- **Swipe right** on an item to reveal the **Export** action, which opens the export dialog. See [Exporting Fits](efa://manual/sharing/exporting-fits).
- **Swipe left** on an item to reveal the **Delete** action; deletion always asks for confirmation first.

## Compatibility Warning

If a fit no longer matches the currently active game data, a warning icon appears on the item together with a hint in the subtitle. See [Channels, Servers & Checkouts](efa://manual/data/channels-servers-checkouts).

## Pull to Refresh

Pull down at the top of the list to reload the local fits.

## Empty State

When there are no fits yet, the page shows a hint. Tap the center plus button or the **Create Fit** shortcut on the workspace to get started.
